Last updated: October 2026.

1. Browsing without an account

You can use the site without signing up or creating an account, and we never ask for personal data.

2. What is stored in your browser

The site stores three choices of yours in your browser: light or dark theme, the temperature unit on the Weather page and, if you give one, your site rating. This stays on your device, is not sent to us, and can be cleared in your browser settings.

3. Third-party services

To show some content, your browser connects directly to other services, which may receive your IP address under their own policies:

  • Open-Meteo: weather and sea conditions.
  • Aladhan: backup prayer times; the main timetable comes from the Ministry of Habous and Islamic Affairs and is stored on the site itself.
  • OpenStreetMap and Google Maps: maps.
  • YouTube (no-cookie mode): when you play a video.
  • Google Fonts: fonts; Blogger: hosting for a few older photos.

4. Analytics and advertising

We may use Google Analytics to count visits. The site shows Google AdSense ads, always marked “Ad”.

  • Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this site and other websites.
  • Google’s advertising cookies enable it and its partners to serve ads that suit you.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of other vendors’ cookies at aboutads.info.
  • Learn more: how Google uses cookies in advertising.

Visitors from the European Economic Area and the UK are asked for consent before advertising cookies are used. We never sell any data.

5. Links and messages

The Facebook and WhatsApp buttons open those apps outside the site, under their own policies. When you message us, we use your message to reply to you.

6. Contact

For any privacy question, use the Contact page.
