Reading the sea in Ksar Sghir

Ksar Sghir sits in the middle of the Strait of Gibraltar, where the wind rules: with the easterly Levante, waves on the beach stay normal; with the westerly Poniente, they get bigger and you need more care, especially with children. In winter the sea is rough on every beach. Above 20 knots the sea gets hard for swimming and small boats.
