Top 3 quiet beaches for families in Ksar Sghir

The top 3 quiet family beaches around Ksar Sghir: Oued Marsa, Dahdaha and Zrara. Family beaches away from the crowds, with facilities, access and tips.

⚡ Quick answer
  1. Oued Marsa: quiet and family-friendly, with restaurants, shops and seasonal kiosks.
  2. Dahdaha: a family cove among the hills, away from the crowds.
  3. Zrara 1 and 2: two family coves among the hills, total calm.

This guide is for families looking for calm and privacy: beaches that don’t get crowded, with a respectful family atmosphere, away from the busy main beaches in August. The selection is based on a local’s experience, and all these beaches have Civil Protection lifeguards from June to September.

🏖️ The three beaches

1. Oued Marsa Beach

⭐ The most complete: family calm plus facilities

  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Very family-friendly
  • 🍽️ Restaurants and shops
  • 🏪 Seasonal kiosks
  • 🌊 Weak currents
  • ⛵ Fishing boats
  • 📶 Weak phone signal

About 12 km from Ksar Sghir and 2 km off the main road, which keeps it quiet. A very family-friendly beach with restaurants, cafés and shops as well as seasonal kiosks, so there’s no need to go far. Pebble beach, traditional fishing boats, weak currents. It belongs to the municipality of Taghramt.

All about Oued Marsa →

2. Dahdaha Beach

⛰️ Calm between hills and sea

  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Very family-friendly
  • ⛰️ Among the hills
  • 🏖️ Sand
  • 🏪 Seasonal kiosks
  • 🚌 I3 and I12

A quiet sandy cove about 15 km west of Ksar Sghir, framed by hills, away from the crowds and the bustle of town. Seasonal kiosks in summer; restaurants and shops are in central Ksar Sghir, so bring food and water for a calm full day.

All about Dahdaha →

3. Zrara Beach 1 and 2

🌿 Two coves for total calm

  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Very family-friendly
  • ⛰️ Among the hills
  • 🏖️ Sand
  • 🏪 Seasonal kiosks
  • 🚌 I3 and I12

Two neighbouring coves about 20 km west of Ksar Sghir, between hills and sea. Two coves means an extra choice: if one fills up, the other stays calm. Seasonal kiosks; restaurants and shops are in central Ksar Sghir, so bring what you need.

🚗 Getting there

  • 🚕
    Oued Marsa: by private taxi or car, paid parking. The bus doesn’t go there.
  • 🚌
    Dahdaha and Zrara: on the coast road towards Tangier, by car or on buses I3 and I12.

🎒 How to prepare

  • 🧺
    For Dahdaha and Zrara: bring food, water and shade; restaurants and shops are in central Ksar Sghir.
  • 📶
    At Oued Marsa: the phone signal is weak, so agree on a meeting point and time in advance.
  • 🌅
    Go in the morning: calmer, and gentler sun for the kids.
  • 🟡
    Swim inside the yellow buoys in the guarded zone.
  • 🌬️
    With the Levante, waves stay normal; with the Poniente, they get bigger and you need more care. See Sea today.
⚠️ Lifeguards from June to September. Follow the flags and the Civil Protection’s instructions, and avoid swimming in winter.

🎈 Looking for kids’ activities instead?

If activities and facilities matter more than calm, these beaches are a better fit:

  • Ksar Sghir Beach: free parasols and kids’ games, in the heart of the area.
  • Sidi Kankouch 1: free parasols, showers and an activity platform.
  • Dalia: Blue Flag, activity platform and a space for people with special needs.
