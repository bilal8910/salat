Ksar Sghir Traditional Fishing Port

Ksar Sghir traditional fishing port: fishermen’s boats, tuna season, fresh Mediterranean fish sold in central Ksar Sghir, and tips for buying fish.

⚡ In short

The traditional fishing port is the heart of Ksar Sghir’s life by the sea. Every day the boats head out into the Strait and come back with fresh Mediterranean fish, sold in central Ksar Sghir. During tuna season the port is especially lively. A place to discover traditional fishing, photograph the boats and buy fresh fish.

  • ⛵ Traditional boats
  • 🐟 Fresh fish daily
  • 🐋 Tuna season
  • 🛒 Sold in central Ksar Sghir
  • 📷 Photography

📍 Location

The fishing port is in Ksar Sghir, on the coast of the Strait of Gibraltar between Tangier and Fnideq. It belongs to the municipality of Ksar Sghir, Fahs-Anjra province. Ksar Sghir beach and the archaeological site are close by, so it fits easily into a walk around the town.

⛵ Traditional fishing, passed down the generations

In Ksar Sghir, fishing is a trade handed down from generation to generation. Fishermen set out in colourful wooden boats into the Strait, where the Mediterranean meets the Atlantic, giving the area a great variety of fish. When the boats return, the port comes alive: crates unloaded, nets mended, fishermen chatting. One of the finest scenes to photograph in Ksar Sghir.

🐋 Tuna season

Bluefin tuna pass through the Strait of Gibraltar on their migration between the Atlantic and the Mediterranean, so Ksar Sghir has a tuna season, when the port is busier than usual. It is a chance for visitors to see a special side of fishing in the Strait and taste fresh tuna in season.

🛒 Where to buy fish

The fish caught by the port’s boats is sold in central Ksar Sghir. There you’ll find fresh fish straight from the sea that same day, depending on the season. You can cook it where you’re staying, or ask a restaurant in the centre to grill it for you.

  • 🌅
    Go early: there’s more choice in the morning.
  • 👀
    Spot fresh fish: bright eyes, red gills, firm flesh.
  • 🧊
    Keep it cool: bring a cool bag in summer.
  • 💬
    Ask the seller about the fish and how best to cook it.

📷 For photographers

Colourful boats, nets, fishermen at work: the port is ideal for photography. The best light is early morning as the boats return, and before sunset when the sea turns golden. Ask before photographing people.

⚠️ Respect the port

The port is a workplace: don’t get in the way of unloading, keep clear of ropes and machinery, and mind the wet ground. Ask before boarding a boat or photographing anyone.

🚗 Getting there

  • 🚌
    By bus from Tangier: lines I12 and I3 from the train station to Ksar Sghir.
  • 🚗
    By car from Tangier: on the A5 motorway or national road 16.
  • 🚕
    From Fnideq: by grand taxi on national road 16.

❓ FAQ

Where is the port’s fish sold?

In central Ksar Sghir, where you’ll find fresh fish straight from the sea that day.

When is tuna season?

Bluefin tuna cross the Strait on their migration, and the port is busier during the season. Ask the fishermen or sellers in town for the dates.

What fish can I find?

Depending on the season: bluefin tuna, sardines, anchovies, sea bream, red mullet, hake, sole, squid, octopus and prawns.

Can I visit the port?

Yes, as long as you respect the fishermen’s work and ask before taking photos or boarding a boat.
