Oued Marsa Beach

Oued Marsa Beach: a family beach through and through, 12 km from Ksar Sghir, weak currents, lifeguards June to September, restaurants, cafés and seasonal kiosks, fishing boats on the shore.

⚡ In short

A family beach through and through, 12 km from Ksar Sghir and 2 km off the main road. Weak currents, lifeguards from June to September, restaurants, cafés and seasonal kiosks, and fishing boats lined up on the shore. A quiet spot, away from the road noise.

  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Family beach
  • 🌊 Weak currents
  • 🛡️ Lifeguards June — Sept
  • 🍽️ Restaurants and cafés
  • ⛵ Fishing boats
  • 📶 Weak phone signal

📍 Location

Oued Marsa Beach lies east of Ksar Sghir, about 12 km away, between Dalia and Belyounech. It sits about 2 km off the main road, which is what keeps it so quiet: no traffic noise, and a feeling of being far away while still close. It belongs to the municipality of Taghramt, Fahs-Anjra province.

Surrounded by green hills, the beach looks out over the Strait of Gibraltar and its ships: mountain and sea in the same view.

🏖️ The beach and the water

The beach is made of pebbles, which keeps the water clear near the shore. What makes it a family beach through and through is its calm, its simple atmosphere and its weak currents.

Even with weak currents, stay alert: swim in the permitted areas, within sight of the Civil Protection. As elsewhere in the area, waves get a little bigger with the Poniente, and swimming is not advised in winter. Check Sea today before you go.

⛵ The fishermen’s boats

One of Oued Marsa’s charms is the row of traditional fishing boats on the beach. The fishermen are part of daily life here, and their colourful boats on the pebbles, with the sea behind, make lovely photos, especially early in the morning.

👍 Who is it for?

  • 👨‍👩‍👧
    Families: the calm and the distance from the road make for a relaxing day for everyone.
  • 📵
    Anyone wanting to unplug: the weak signal turns into a chance for a real day with family and the sea.
  • 📸
    Photographers: colourful boats, pebbles and green hills make a change from sandy beaches.
  • 🍽️
    Food lovers: restaurants and cafés are close, so there is no need to leave all day.

🌿 The place and the view

The small road down from the main road to the beach, about 2 km long, winds between the hills to a quiet cove. In spring the hills are green; in summer the blue sea meets the pebbles and the colourful boats, a scene regulars to the area love.

👨‍👩‍👧 For families

Oued Marsa is one of the beaches families pick for peace and quiet: away from the road, weak currents, restaurants and cafés close by. For a smooth day:

  • 👟
    Water shoes: the pebbles make walking barefoot tiring for children.
  • 📍
    Agree on a meeting point with the kids: the signal is weak, so you can’t rely on phones.
  • 👀
    Stay close to the kids in the water, in the permitted areas.

🚗 Getting there

  • 🚗
    From Ksar Sghir (12 km): on national road 16 towards Fnideq, then about 2 km from the main road down to the beach.
  • 🚗
    A car is the easiest way, as the beach is set back from the main road.

In summer, early morning is the best time: a quiet beach and fishing boats coming back from sea. June and September are calmer than August, with lifeguards on duty.

🗓️ A suggested day

  • 🌅
    Morning: photos of the boats on the pebbles, then a swim in calm water.
  • 🍽️
    Lunch: at one of the restaurants near the beach.
  • ☕
    Afternoon: a break in a café facing the sea.
  • 🌇
    Late afternoon: a drive to nearby Dalia or Belyounech.

🎒 What to bring

  • Water shoes, because of the pebbles.
  • A parasol, water and sunscreen.
  • Cash: the signal is weak and some payments may not go through.
  • A thick towel or mat to sit on the pebbles.

⚠️ Safety

Follow the flags and the Civil Protection’s instructions and swim in the permitted areas. Take extra care with the Poniente and avoid swimming in winter. Outside June — September there are no lifeguards.

❓ FAQ

Where is Oued Marsa Beach?

About 12 km east of Ksar Sghir, between Dalia and Belyounech, 2 km off the main road. It belongs to the municipality of Taghramt.

Is the beach good for families?

Yes, it is a family beach through and through: quiet, weak currents, restaurants and cafés close by.

Is it a sandy beach?

It is a pebble beach, so water shoes are a good idea.

Is the beach lifeguarded?

Yes, from June to September.

Are there restaurants?

Yes, restaurants and cafés nearby, plus seasonal kiosks in summer.

Does my phone work there?

The signal is weak on the beach, so make plans before you arrive.
