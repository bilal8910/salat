Dahdaha Beach

Dahdaha Beach: a quiet sandy cove 15 km west of Ksar Sghir, a family beach framed by hills, lifeguards June to September, seasonal kiosks, buses I3 and I12.

📍 Location

Dahdaha Beach lies about 15 km west of Ksar Sghir, between Ferdiwa and Oued Alian, on a stretch of coast where the hills come down to the sea. It belongs to the municipality of Ksar Sghir, Fahs-Anjra province.

🏖️ The beach

A small sandy cove, about 100 to 400 metres long, surrounded by green hills. There are seasonal kiosks in summer; restaurants and shops are in central Ksar Sghir, so most visitors bring their own food and water for the day.

⛰️ A beach framed by hills

Dahdaha is a true family beach, with hills on one side and the sea on the other, in a landscape of green and blue. This natural setting gives it a special calm, perfect for a whole day with the family between swimming, relaxing on the sand and taking in the view. The loveliest time is early morning, when the light falls on the hills and the water.

🌬️ Levante and Poniente

With the Levante, waves stay normal and good for swimming. With the Poniente, they get bigger, so stay close to the kids in the water. In winter the hills-and-sea view is still lovely for walks and photos, but not for swimming. Check Sea today before you go.

🛡️ Summer facilities

🛡️
Civil Protection
Lifeguards from June to September, like the other beaches in the area.
🏪
Seasonal kiosks

👨‍👩‍👧 For families

  • 🌅
    Go in the morning: a calmer beach and gentler sun for the kids.
  • 📍
    Agree on a meeting point with the children, such as the lifeguard post.
  • 👀
    Stay close to the kids in the water, especially on Poniente days.
  • 🧺
    For a full day: bring food, water and a parasol; restaurants and shops are in central Ksar Sghir.

🚗 Getting there

  • 🚌
    By bus from Tangier: lines I3 and I12 from the train station run along the coast road past the beach.
  • 🚗
    From Ksar Sghir (15 km): on national road 16 towards Tangier.

⚠️ Visit, not swimming

Early morning is the most beautiful time, when the light reaches the hills and the water and the beach is at its calmest. June and September suit anyone who wants calm with lifeguards on duty.

⚠️ Safety

Follow the flags and the Civil Protection’s instructions and swim in the guarded zone. Take extra care with the Poniente and avoid swimming in winter. Outside June — September there are no lifeguards.

❓ FAQ

Where is Dahdaha Beach?

About 15 km west of Ksar Sghir, on the coast road towards Tangier; it belongs to the municipality of Ksar Sghir.

Are there restaurants at Dahdaha Beach?

There are seasonal kiosks in summer. Restaurants and shops are in central Ksar Sghir, so bring food and water.

Is the beach lifeguarded?

Yes, from June to September.

Can I get there by bus?

Yes, lines I3 and I12 from Tangier train station pass right by.
