Sidi Kankouch 1 Beach

Sidi Kankouch 1 Beach: wide sand and weak currents 10 km from Tangier, free parasols, showers and an activity platform in summer, lifeguards June to September, buses I3 and I12.

⚡ In short

A wide sandy beach with weak currents, about 10 km from Tangier and 25 km (30 minutes) from Ksar Sghir. In summer: lifeguards from June to September, free parasols, showers and toilets, seasonal kiosks and restaurants, and an activity platform for kids and adults. Served by buses I3 and I12 from Tangier.

  • 🛡️ Lifeguards June — Sept
  • 🌊 Weak currents
  • ⛱️ Free parasols
  • 🚿 Showers and toilets
  • 🎤 Activity platform
  • 🚌 I3 and I12 from Tangier

📍 Location

Sidi Kankouch 1 Beach lies on the coast road between Tangier and Ksar Sghir, west of Ksar Sghir towards Tangier. It is about 10 km from Tangier and 25 km from central Ksar Sghir, around 30 minutes by car. It belongs to the municipality of Ksar Sghir, Fahs-Anjra province.

Being so close to Tangier makes it a favourite with city residents looking for a calm sea away from the central beaches, without a long drive. Right next to it is Sidi Kankouch 2 Beach.

🏖️ The beach and the water

The beach is sandy and fairly wide, with room for families even on busy days. Its big advantage: weak currents, which make swimming comfortable, especially for children.

As elsewhere in the area, waves stay normal with the Levante and get a little bigger with the Poniente, which is when to keep a closer eye on children. In winter, swimming is not advised. Check Sea today before you go.

👨‍👩‍👧 For families

Sidi Kankouch 1 is one of the most convenient beaches in the area for families: wide sand, weak currents, free parasols, showers and toilets and an activity platform, all in one place. For a smooth day:

  • 🌅
    Go in the morning: more free parasols available and easier parking.
  • 📍
    Agree on a meeting point with the children, such as the activity platform or the lifeguard post.
  • 👀
    Stay close to the kids in the water, especially on Poniente days.

🚗 Getting there

  • 🚌
    By bus from Tangier: lines I3 and I12 from the train station pass by the beach.
  • 🚗
    From Tangier by car (about 10 km): along the coast road towards Ksar Sghir.
  • 🚗
    From Ksar Sghir (25 km, 30 minutes): on national road 16 towards Tangier.

⚠️ Visit, not swimming

Close to Tangier, the beach fills up mainly at weekends, especially in August. June, September and weekdays are the calmest, with all facilities open.

🗓️ A suggested day

  • 🌅
    Morning: a swim in calm water and pick of a free parasol.
  • 🎨
    Late morning: kids at the drawing area or the competitions.
  • 🍽️
    Lunch: at the seasonal kiosks and restaurants.
  • 🌇
    Late afternoon: a look at neighbouring Sidi Kankouch 2, then back to Tangier before the traffic.

🎒 What to bring

  • Water, sunscreen and hats for the children.
  • A towel and a change of clothes.
  • Small change for parking and the kiosks.

⚠️ Safety

Follow the flags and the Civil Protection’s instructions and swim in the guarded zone. Take extra care with the Poniente and avoid swimming in winter. Outside June — September there are no lifeguards.

❓ FAQ

How far is Sidi Kankouch 1 from Tangier?

About 10 km along the coast road towards Ksar Sghir.

Can I get there by bus?

Yes, lines I3 and I12 from Tangier train station pass by the beach.

Are the parasols free?

Yes, free parasols are available in summer.

Are there showers and toilets?

Yes, in summer.

Is the beach good for children?

Yes: wide sand, weak currents, an activity platform and a drawing area.

Is parking free?

Parking is paid.
