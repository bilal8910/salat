Welcome to Ksar Sghir

Ksar Sghir (also spelled Ksar es-Seghir or Ksar Seghir) is a small coastal town in Fahs-Anjra province, about 35 km east of Tangier, right on the Strait of Gibraltar. On clear days you can see the Spanish coast from its beach, and ships cross the Strait all day long.

Around it are beaches for every taste: Blue Flag Dalia, Sidi Kankouch with its services and activities, and calm family coves such as Dahdaha, Zrara and Oued Marsa. A few minutes away are Tanger Med port, the archaeological site in the heart of town and the village of Belyounech below Jebel Musa.

This guide covers what you need to visit Ksar Sghir, Morocco: every beach, port and historic site, daily sea conditions, ferry times and useful numbers, with photos and tips from someone who lives here.
